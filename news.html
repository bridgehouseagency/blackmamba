Match Report
September 8, 2026

Mambas Fight Back to Beat Riverside United 3–1

A second-half masterclass from the midfield turned the game around at home.

Trailing 1-0 at the break, Mamba FC came out with renewed energy, scoring three unanswered goals through Isaac Baptiste (2) and Kwame Asante. Head Coach Anthony Mensah praised the squad's discipline and the home crowd's support as key factors in the turnaround.

Club Announcement
September 2, 2026

Youth Development Camp Registration Now Open

Mamba FC launches a free youth camp for players aged 8–14 this fall.

In partnership with the New Brunswick African Association, the club is opening a six-week youth development camp focused on fundamentals, teamwork, and confidence-building. Spaces are limited — sign up through the club office or contact us directly.

Community
August 26, 2026

Mamba FC Hosts Community Family Day at the Pitch

Families gathered for food, music, and a friendly exhibition match.

Over 200 community members joined the club's annual Family Day, celebrating culture and connection through football. Proceeds from the event support the club's youth equipment fund.

Match Report
August 30, 2026

Narrow Cup Defeat to Fredericton Rovers

A late goal ended the Mambas' cup run on the road, 2–1.

Despite a spirited performance and an early goal from Chidi Eze, Mamba FC fell to a late strike in the 88th minute. The squad now turns its focus back to the league campaign.

Player Feature
August 10, 2026

Rising Star: Victor Achebe Named Player of the Month

The young winger's pace and creativity earned him August's top honour.

Since joining the youth-to-senior pathway last year, Victor Achebe has been a standout performer, recording 4 goals and 5 assists this season. "This club believed in me before I believed in myself," he said.
