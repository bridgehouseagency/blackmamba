About Mamba FC

Building character, community, and champions — one player at a time.

Our Story

Rooted in Community, Driven by Passion

Mamba FC was founded to give young players in our community a place to belong, compete, and grow. Wearing black, green, and red, our teams represent discipline, growth, and heart on every pitch we play on.

From weekly training sessions to competitive league matches, Mamba FC is committed to developing not just better footballers, but better people.

Purpose

Our Vision & Mission

🟢 Our Vision

To promote young talent and prepare them for the future by nurturing and developing the next generation of footballers — creating opportunities for players to reach their full potential on and off the field.

🔴 Our Mission

To provide a safe, supportive, and competitive environment where young athletes build technical skill, discipline, teamwork, and confidence — while celebrating and strengthening our community through the game of football.

Our Organization

Managed by the New Brunswick African Association

NBAA

Mamba FC is proudly managed by the New Brunswick African Association, an organization dedicated to supporting and uplifting the African community across New Brunswick through culture, education, and sport.

Through Mamba FC, the association creates pathways for young people to develop athletic skill, leadership, and a strong sense of belonging — on the field and within the wider community.
